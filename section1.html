The four eukaryotic groups

Plants, animals, fungi and protoctists. Every group is defined by a short list of features — cell walls, chloroplasts, how they feed, how they store carbohydrate. Marks are lost by mixing up the cell wall materials.

Prokaryotes, pathogens and viruses

Bacteria are prokaryotic: no nucleus, a circular chromosome, plasmids. Viruses are not living organisms at all, which is why they sit apart from every group on this page.

Pathogens

What a pathogen is1.4
  • A pathogen is an organism that causes disease.
  • Pathogens may be fungi, bacteria, protoctists or viruses — not one group alone.
  • Being a pathogen is a way of living, not a taxonomic group. Most members of each group are harmless.
